Last Updated: February 6, 2026

Welcome to QuickTools! We built this site to be a safe and helpful resource for students, teachers, and creators. By using our website, you agree to these rules. We've written them in plain language so they are easy for everyone to understand.

1 Our Privacy Promise

QuickTools is a "Privacy-First" zone. This is our core commitment to you:

  • Local Processing: All tools run inside your browser. Your files never leave your device.
  • No Accounts: You don't need to sign up or give us an email to use our tools.
  • Safe for All: We comply with safety standards for students and young creators.

2 How to Use QuickTools

You are encouraged to:

  • Use these tools for school assignments and homework.
  • Create graphics for your club, team, or personal hobbies.
  • Use our QR and Barcode generators for your own projects.

You must not:

  • Use these tools to create harmful, mean, or illegal content.
  • Attempt to "hack" the site or break the code that runs the tools.
  • Create "fake" identification documents or misleading information.

3 Ownership of Work

You own 100% of what you create. QuickTools does not claim any rights to your images, PDFs, or codes. You are simply using our "digital hammer" to build your own "digital house."

While we try to make our tools perfect, we provide them "as is." This means we aren't responsible if a tool doesn't work exactly how you expected for a specific project. Always double-check your work!

Have a Question?

If you or your parents are unsure about these rules, please use the "Support" link on our main page to send us a message.
